Changes by the ZIME contributors (2026): indexed simplified/traditional
headwords; removed reference-only senses; ranked ordinary senses before
bound forms and proper names; generated a bounded reverse English index.
The runtime additionally filters explicit regional usage labels and projects
the mixed Mainland/Taiwan note for 你/妳 into region-specific definitions.
These projected definition strings also remain under CC BY-SA 4.0.
Index revision 2 preserves traditional/simplified headword identity and source
pronunciation. Candidate annotations coalesce duplicate displayed glosses and
separate explanatory notes/references from distinguishing usage qualifiers;
the original regional definitions remain available in details.
