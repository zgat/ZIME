Changes by the ZIME contributors (2026): indexed simplified/traditional
headwords; preserved all original source senses and pronunciation identities;
ranked common readings before proper names; generated a bounded reverse English index.
The runtime additionally prefers explicit regional usage labels and projects
the mixed Mainland/Taiwan note for 你/妳 into region-specific definitions.
These projected definition strings also remain under CC BY-SA 4.0.
Index revision 3 retains 124,988 complete source entries / 199,654 source senses
and generates annotations for 197,976 spellings using the shared build/runtime
parser in sources/ZIMELocalLexicon.swift. Meanings, annotations and explicit
references are classified separately; alias/abbreviation targets are resolved
with spelling/reading identity and cycle/depth guards. Ordinary English beginning
with words such as "see" is preserved. Parentheses are classified by content,
preserving semantic qualifiers, complete grammatical meanings and unrecognized
content rather than deleting every parenthesis. Duplicate core glosses coalesce;
full source definitions and reference notes remain in optional hover details.
Candidates are looked up by their own spelling regardless of the active script;
regional preferences fall back to other available senses rather than blocking
a translation.
